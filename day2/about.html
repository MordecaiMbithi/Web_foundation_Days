About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help students and developers organize their daily ideas without distraction. It provides a clean, immediate space to draft reminders, manage checklist items, and keep track of project thoughts in real time.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought or reminder into the "New note" field on the homepage.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to add it to your active list.
  3. Review your saved items anytime under the "Your notes" section.

Features

  • Instant note creation with zero setup required
  • Accessible semantic layout optimized for screen readers
  • Minimalist, distraction-free reading experience
  • Fast navigation between documentation and active workspace
